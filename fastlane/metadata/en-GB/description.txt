KARINEX is the app of the online shop karinex.de. It offers digital licence keys for software and a few physical media.

THE RANGE
Every product, price, description and image comes straight from the shop. You see the prices and texts of your country, in your language.

EASY ORDERING
Add products to your basket and pay in the secure Shopify checkout with Apple Pay, card or Klarna. The app itself never handles payment data.

YOUR ACCOUNT
Sign in with your customer account to see your orders and their status, download invoices, manage your addresses and keep delivered licence keys protected on this device, optionally with Face ID.

HELP
Our customer service is available by WhatsApp, email and live chat, Monday to Sunday from 06:00 to 23:00 German time. Activation guides are shown with each product and in our guides.

PRIVACY
No tracking, no ads. Analytics and notifications only with your explicit consent.

Available in 11 languages.
